BEFORE YOU BUY

Is your phone eSIM ready?

Most newer iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel models support eSIM. Your phone must also be network-unlocked.

HELP CENTRE

Good to know.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. Your usual SIM remains in your phone; Nila Mobile provides the travel data connection.

When does my plan begin?

The launch plan begins on its first successful connection in the destination. Always check the activation rule shown with your plan.

Do I receive a phone number?

These are data-only travel plans unless a plan explicitly says otherwise. Calls can continue through apps such as WhatsApp.

What happens after payment?

Your eSIM installation details and QR code will be delivered to your email address.
