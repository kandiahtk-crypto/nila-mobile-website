A QUICK CHECK BEFORE BUYING

Is your phone ready for eSIM?

Most recent iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel models support eSIM. Your device must also be network-unlocked.

HELP CENTRE

Questions, answered clearly.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. Keep your usual SIM in your phone and use Nila Mobile for travel data.

When does my plan begin?

This plan begins on its first successful connection in Sri Lanka.

Does it include calls and texts?

No. It is data-only. Use internet apps such as WhatsApp for calls and messages.

What happens after payment?

Your installation details and QR code are sent to the email address provided with your order.
