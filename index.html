Before you buy

Is your phone ready for eSIM?

Support can vary by exact model, country of purchase and mobile network. Check the settings on your own phone and confirm it is unlocked.

Apple iPhone

Open Settings → Mobile Service or Cellular. Look for “Add eSIM”. Under General → About, check for an EID and your network lock status.

Android phones

Open Settings → Connections or Network & internet → SIM manager. Look for “Add eSIM” or “Download a SIM”. Names vary by device.

Still unsure?

Confirm eSIM support and unlocking with your manufacturer or home network before paying. Email hello@nilamobile.com for plan help.

A little help before take-off

When does my plan start?

Activation rules vary by package. Check the plan information and delivery instructions; do not assume every plan starts only on arrival.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

You can keep your existing WhatsApp account while using the eSIM for data. Choose to keep your current number if prompted. Home-network calls, texts and roaming may still incur charges.

How will I receive my eSIM?

After successful payment, the fulfilment system processes your eSIM and sends installation details to your checkout email. Allow processing time and check your junk folder. Contact support if delivery is missing.

Can I buy several eSIMs?

Save plans in your bag to compare them. This version uses a separate secure checkout for each eSIM. Complete a new purchase for every additional eSIM you need.

Does every plan include 5G or hotspot?

These features depend on the selected package, supported network and device. Review the supplier’s package information and ask support if a feature is essential.
