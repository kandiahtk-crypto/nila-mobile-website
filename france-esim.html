Regional touring and longer stays

Think about your whole route: a Paris weekend, Provence stay and Alps journey can involve different patterns of use. Check the listed network for the areas you plan to visit.

Borders and overseas destinations

A France plan does not automatically include neighbouring countries or overseas territories. If you will visit Monaco, Andorra or another country, check whether it is specifically included.

Coverage and activation: check the package

Read the listed network

The available plan cards show network information returned by the catalogue. Check the exact package for your route. Coverage and speeds vary by location, device and network conditions; 5G and hotspot are not universal features.

Check when validity begins

Do not assume installation and activation are the same event. Follow your chosen package’s activation rules and delivery instructions. If the start trigger is unclear, ask support before installing.

Keep your home SIM costs in view

Choose the travel eSIM as your data line when ready and follow the package’s roaming instructions. Your home provider may charge separately for calls, texts or roaming if that line remains active.

Need a specific network, hotspot, local SMS or coverage at a particular address? Ask before checkout. We do not infer these features from a country name.

How much data should you choose for France?

Maps and messaging

Start with the number of travel days and your usual mobile usage. Offline maps and Wi-Fi downloads can reduce the data you need while away.

Photos, video and work

Allow more headroom for video calls, social uploads, cloud backups or tethering. App settings and video quality can change usage substantially.

Your phone’s recent data-use history is a better starting point than a universal “GB per day” promise. Match both allowance and validity to the visit, and check whether top-ups are available rather than assuming they are.

Before you buy

Is your phone ready for eSIM?

Support can vary by exact model, country of purchase and mobile network. Check the settings on your own phone and confirm it is unlocked.

Apple iPhone

Open Settings → Mobile Service or Cellular. Look for “Add eSIM”. Under General → About, check for an EID and your network lock status.

Android phones

Open Settings → Connections or Network & internet → SIM manager. Look for “Add eSIM” or “Download a SIM”. Names vary by device.

Still unsure?

Confirm eSIM support and unlocking with your manufacturer or home network before paying. Email hello@nilamobile.com for plan help.

A little help before take-off

Will my France eSIM work elsewhere in Europe?

Only where the package explicitly lists those countries. A France-only plan and a regional Europe plan can have different coverage. Compare the actual included destinations before choosing.

When does my plan start?

Activation rules vary by package. Check the plan information and delivery instructions; do not assume every plan starts only on arrival.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

You can keep your existing WhatsApp account while using the eSIM for data. Choose to keep your current number if prompted. Home-network calls, texts and roaming may still incur charges.

How will I receive my eSIM?

After successful payment, the fulfilment system processes your eSIM and sends installation details to your checkout email. Allow processing time and check your junk folder. Contact support if delivery is missing.

Can I buy several eSIMs?

Save plans in your bag to compare them. This version uses a separate secure checkout for each eSIM. Complete a new purchase for every additional eSIM you need.

Does every plan include 5G or hotspot?

These features depend on the selected package, supported network and device. Review the supplier’s package information and ask support if a feature is essential.
