How much travel eSIM data do you need?

Choose an allowance around how you actually use your phone and how long you will be away. There is no single daily data figure that fits every traveller.

Start with your own usage

Look at recent mobile-data use in your phone settings and note the reporting period. Separate work or home habits from what you expect to do on the trip. A short city break and a long family visit may need different allowances.

Group your main activities

  • Navigation and messages: allow for route searches, travel updates and messaging. Download maps and essential booking information before departure.
  • Social media and photos: uploads, autoplay and cloud backups can use data in the background. Review your app settings.
  • Video calls, streaming and work: quality settings, call length and large files make a substantial difference. Check hotspot support if you intend to connect a laptop.

Check allowance and validity together

A larger allowance is not helpful if its validity does not cover your visit. Check when the package starts and when it expires. Do not assume unused data rolls over or that more data can be added later.

Leave a practical margin

Keep some headroom for changes to your journey. Use trusted Wi-Fi for large downloads where appropriate, and monitor usage on your phone and any plan-status information provided with the package.

Ask about essential features

Before paying, confirm anything that matters to your trip: a particular network, hotspot, a local phone number or coverage in an outlying area. A destination name alone does not establish these features.
